# Strategic state and control

The fleet simulation exposes three state axes and four control families. Detailed
stocks, ships, communities, institutions, and records remain causal simulation
objects; they do not become additional global currencies.

`Fleet_Stock` is the sole material authority. Older scalar reserve, material,
and Propellant mirrors are not simulation inputs. Every production, consumption,
commitment, recovery, reward, and loss passes through the typed fleet ledger
with a causal event reference.

Routine commitments preserve an operating floor: the agricultural food floor,
two seasons of manufactured-goods and service maintenance, 12 Supplies plus
the return allocation of an active expedition, and its protected Propellant reserve. An explicit
emergency decision may cross a floor, but must name the exposed capability.

| Control | Reserves | Capacity | Cohesion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allocation | Release or protect consumables | Assign ships, facilities, and specialists | Distribute hardship and access |
| Policy | Set floors, rationing, and replenishment priorities | Set operating and risk priorities | Set procedures and acceptable treatment |
| Organization | Give institutions custody of stockpiles | Give institutions control of capabilities | Establish legitimate authority and representation |
| Commitment | Promise future material support | Reserve future work and ships | Stake trust on delivery |

Information overlays the matrix. A forecast or consequence can be known,
bounded, disputed, or unknown. Knowledge is persistent evidence or capability;
analysis requires ships, specialists, compute, institutions, and time rather than
spending a global Knowledge score.

## Passage interface

A Passage contract transfers Supplies into a material manifest and separately reserves Compute,
Manpower, and Raw Materials capacity. Reservations are conserved, reject typed
bottleneck over-allocation, and are released exactly once at settlement. Course
actions consume manifest Supplies. A fleet return recovers unused Supplies;
relay conclusions and missing or lost voyages do not return them. Discovered
rewards are accounted separately from recovered allocations.
The simulation derives:

- **Range** from allocated reserves and tankage;
- **Support** from allocated reserves and support ships;
- **Readiness** from ship condition and allocated capacity;
- **Information** from survey, archive, route, and site records;
- **Authority** from Cohesion, institutions, ship autonomy, and command capability;
- **Hold capacity** from the selected hulls.

These are expedition conditions, not currencies owned independently of the fleet.
Changing the task group or the political state changes the derived manifest.

Cohesion is an effective reading, not only a fleet-wide average. Baseline
cohesion is modified by population-weighted community trust and by divergence,
so a severely aggrieved minority remains strategically consequential.
Contradicted or withheld accounts remain Disputed only until exposed; survey or
archive capability determines whether otherwise unresolved information is Known
or Unknown. Reserve forecasts derive their setback and uncertainty buffers from
the objective, risk policy, selected doctrine and ships, and discovered sites.
