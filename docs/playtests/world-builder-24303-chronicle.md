# World Builder chronicle — seed 24303

Run with:

```sh
build/dev/last-best-hope --simulate 1 world-builder --story 24303
```

The campaign ran for 53 seasons and ended **Fragmented Survival · Maintained**.
It founded one settlement, Moss Cistern, with three ships; four ships were
lost, six were scarred, and no bot action was invalid.

## Record

- Season 0: the fleet referred its founding charter to affected
  representatives. The resulting precedent required that binding decisions be
  referred to those representatives.
- Season 4: the fleet opened a settlement proposal for Moss Cistern. Three
  ships entered the founding decision, and Moss Cistern was founded under the
  recorded Council Assignment procedure. The Council recorded both the
  assignment and the settlement's independent authority.
- Season 8: GMS Blue Finch received priority repairs; its damage fell by two.
- Seasons 10–17: GMS The Broad Bridge accumulated damage, received a permanent
  hull breach, entered mothball custody, and required a recovery or role
  substitution commitment. GMS Public Hand also received a permanent hull
  breach and was abandoned in season 17; emergency crews recovered raw
  materials and equipment.
- Season 18: Moss Cistern first deferred maintenance after fleet support was
  not funded.
- Seasons 19–24: GMS Measure Nine was scarred, repaired after repeated
  maintenance failure, and later abandoned. Settlement independence entered
  the public record.
- Seasons 26–39: GMS Signal Three and GMS Mercy Watches each accumulated
  repeated maintenance damage; both were repaired once and later abandoned.
  Moss Cistern continued to report and repeatedly deferred maintenance.
- Seasons 41–53: the Navigation Guild's route-staffing petitions went
  unanswered. The Ocean Memory recorded its jurisdiction and representation
  petitions as unanswered, entered opposition to the Navigation Guild, and
  GMS Measure Seven's operational jurisdiction remained disputed.
- Season 51: cohesion pressure exceeded the operating margin. The fleet
  continued under contested authority and adopted Institutional Recovery as a
  structural response.
- Season 53: GMS Garden and Dawn Successor was damaged, then preserved with
  diverted equipment and services. Moss Cistern again deferred maintenance.
  The three-season finale concluded as Fragmented Survival · Maintained.
