# Continuous Dark expeditions

## Purpose

Passage is the operational expedition half of *Last Best Hope*. A persistent task group leaves the fleet to answer an existing need, navigates a continuous four-dimensional environment, crosses stable correspondences to generated galaxy locations, and returns evidence that can change institutional doctrine.

The Dark has no authored sites or route graph. Static topology, folds, reefs, gradients, and doors are generated deterministically from the campaign seed and 4D chunk coordinates. Ecology, weather, wakes, injuries, access, and observations change only while an expedition is active and unpaused.

## Spatial model

Simulation positions are `(x, y, z, w)`. Distance, sensing, collision, feeding, and locomotion use the local smooth 4D metric rather than projected screen distance. Folds alter continuous travel distance and direction; they never teleport an organism.

The player sees three spatial bearings plus relative inward or outward displacement. There is no universal, player-facing `w` coordinate. A body can overlap in the visible projection without touching in 4D, or approach through fourth-axis separation without first crossing the visible plane.

Depth is separation from the expedition's current anchor correspondence. Ships pay increasing coherence, law-drift, and sensor costs with depth. Adapted organisms gain fourth-axis acceleration, turning authority, and reachable folded paths, but remain constrained by anatomy, sensing, energy, environmental tolerance, and elapsed time.

## Compact undertaking and charter

Passage begins only from an accepted Expeditionary Compact call. Ships and
resources are temporarily seconded by named contributors; accepting the call
reserves those contributions exactly once. The manifest distinguishes reserved,
consumed, recovered, and lost stock. Course cost consumes stock
deterministically, and only unused stock physically returned to the fleet is
released. Ending at an authenticated relay preserves the report but not the
fleet's manifest stock.

The active `Operation_Charter` is the sole operational contract. It records:

- hard authority and its actor/event provenance;
- the undertaking intent and accepted exposure;
- each contributor's ship, resources, conditions, and disclosure expectations;
- standing rescue, exposure, withdrawal, disclosure, and communications doctrine.

Passage reads its objective and required capabilities directly from that
charter. The Compact may redirect the intent or withdraw in good faith during
the operation; the report records the change, its communication timing, and
the contributors exposed by it.

Only one Dark expedition fleet may be active. Discoveries remain local until a fleet debrief or authenticated relay upload.

## Strategy and institutional learning

A strategy profile independently selects:

- shallow, balanced, or deep posture;
- shortest-metric, best-mapped, or lowest-coherence course priority;
- avoidant, balanced, or contact-tolerant ecology posture;
- relay-first or objective-first communications posture;
- conservative, balanced, or mission-first withdrawal margin.

This produces 162 complete profiles. Standing doctrine recommends one using
purpose-specific records, the charter's authority, contributor expectations,
and rescue policy. Fixed neutral Bayesian priors keep early evidence uncertain.
Objective completion, safe conclusion, record recovery, losses, damage, time,
course cost, depth, and environment remain separate facts rather than a
universal score.

Recommendations set defaults and advice only. They never alter encounter probability, ecology, or hidden geography. Players may override them; a contradiction with centralized authority is recorded and can affect legitimacy or ship autonomy.

Missing voyages remain unresolved observations. Confirmed loss resolves the original record, and late authenticated evidence revises that record in place. A late report never creates a duplicate attempt.

## Navigation

Players author continuous 4D course polylines. Forecasts report metric distance, ship time, membrane time, field depth, coherence exposure, topology confidence, weather, and observed ecological interception risk. Dark movement is cheap; every expedition instead carries a field-depth rating that defines its stable operating band. A deeper correspondence may be entered only through an explicit emergency commitment, must end at a detected door, and can leave the expedition reliant on a relay or a recoverable missing-voyage record. Dark membrane time integrates `exp(-depth)` over the duration experienced aboard, so deeper routes leave less time in the fleet-facing universe. Chronicle records made during a voyage retain both clocks and the depth of the event. Autopilot follows the course with bounded local 4D avoidance. Material obstruction, dangerous contact, coherence thresholds, objective evidence, unknown doors, and arrival pause the simulation for a new decision.

The expedition selects a sensor posture independently of its course. Quiet
running reduces range, confidence, wake, and update cadence. Passive sensing is
the baseline. Active sweeps improve the fix while adding coherence exposure and
emissions. Illumination provides the strongest anatomical and behavioral
evidence, but nearby organisms can respond to the emission. Posture never
creates a contact or changes hidden geography; it changes only observation and
the response of bodies already present.

Doors are bounded 4D correspondence volumes. Their galaxy endpoint is permanent for the campaign seed and remains institutionally unknown until a crossing record returns. Crossing pauses in normal space for explicit next-leg selection; it never ends or redirects the mission automatically.

Interstellar legs use mapped Dark correspondences. Direct relativistic
normal-space legs are unavailable. Once an expedition leaves the Dark, it may
re-enter through an accessible local door and route toward the moving fleet's
published endpoint. The physical propellant aboard its selected ships remains
escrowed from the main fleet until recovery or loss.

A mission normally ends when the expedition returns home to the fleet. If every mapped route home becomes inaccessible, an expedition at an authenticated relay may transmit its record and remain there; those ships leave the traveling fleet with preserved history.

Separated relay expeditions remain living campaign actors rather than losses. Once per season they maintain the relay, repair damage within their own capacity, and make deterministic progress toward reopening a correspondence. The chronicle records only meaningful thresholds: signal detection, localization, a forecast transit window, and an established route. Survey ships accelerate this work, existing atlas knowledge and relay condition help, and damage delays it; progress does not reset.

When a route opens, the fleet transmits a standing invitation to return, but the separated ships decide. Their decision is deterministic and follows their recorded circumstances: time away, relay viability, ship roles and traits, damage, fleet promises, and enacted ship-autonomy precedents. The player acknowledges the report rather than commanding the outcome. Returning ships preserve their damage, scars, relationships, and histories. Ships that remain continue as a named remote community rather than being recorded as destroyed. An active separated expedition prevents automatic fleet-dissolution resolution while its route search remains open.

## Ecology

The initial web contains:

- Wake film: aggregate primary life consuming wake energy.
- Lantern grazers: embodied 4D organisms consuming film.
- Hush colonies: fields and bodies that can attach to grazers or ship boundaries.
- Shear hunters: embodied deep-adapted predators.
- Grave reefs: persistent habitat bodies supporting shelter and succession.

Embodied organisms have stable identities, 4D position and velocity, SDF genomes, energy, condition, injury masks, sensory profiles, mobility tolerances, behavior, targets, and manifestation history. Consequential contacts resolve against those bodies. Abundant small life remains an aggregate 4D field.

Manifestation is observer-dependent and separate from biological motion. Entry, separation, reconnection, and withdrawal are slices of coherent 4D anatomy, including injury and the finite visible interval after death. Named observations, ship symbionts, contact procedures, scars, wakes, reefs, and transmitted records persist.

Organisms act only on local correspondence evidence. They cannot infer unrestricted intercepts for independently detached vessels.

## Player information

The operational display provides depth bands, fourth-axis entry and withdrawal marks, topology and gradient overlays, course forecasts, doors, local tracks, and the fleet correspondence atlas. A detected body exposes provisional identity, relative 4D bearing, velocity, estimated extent, preferred depth, behavior, target, energy and condition bands, injury, manifestation history, and confidence.

Course and contact forecasts carry bounded causal factors marked observed,
inferred, or unknown. The display exposes principal contributors and their
provenance without revealing unobserved identities, positions, or topology.

The ecology has no separate operation menu. It matters through navigation, contact, observation, damage, symbiosis, and institutional evidence.

## Determinism and persistence

Dark advancement uses fixed deterministic steps and stable entity ordering. Each step updates environment, wakes, production, sensing and intent, movement, contact and feeding, injury, reproduction and death, then observations. Pausing prevents subsequent requested steps.

Campaign format v8 persists dynamic chunk alterations, doors and atlas
provenance, courses and dual-clock telemetry, organisms and fields, relays,
voyage records, strategy statistics, ship-specific Dark history, Compact
resource ledgers, intent changes, archived undertakings, counsel, and callbacks.
Every pre-v8 campaign is explicitly incompatible and has no semantic migration.

## Implementation boundary

`packages/game/dark_continuum.odin` owns deterministic 4D chunks, metric
queries, fields, folds, doors, reefs, organisms, SDF contact, manifestations,
and fixed-step ecology. `packages/game/passage.odin` owns contracts, sponsor
strategy, course execution, correspondence entry, knowledge, relays, conclusions,
and institutional learning. `src/continuous_dark_map.odin` presents those rules
and samples manifested anatomy; it does not own simulation state.

The graphical briefing and line-delimited JSON agent protocol expose the same
strategy dimensions and legal actions. Headless simulation, soak, matched-tempo,
balance, and release-validation commands dispatch through the same game rules.

Verification must cover generation-order determinism, fixed-step replay, 4D
separation and contact, depth mobility, manifestations, ecology, doors,
contracts, learning, persistence, relays, and save compatibility. Static checks
must find no runtime graph-era Passage types or documentation that presents an
authored route graph as authoritative.
